# Summary
The September 2023 Balancer Boosted Pool incident involved a critical weakness in the accounting of ERC-4626 Linear Pools used as components of Boosted Pools. The vulnerable implementation was `ERC4626LinearPool`. Its wrapped-token rate is obtained from the ERC-4626 vault through `convertToAssets()` and scaled for token decimals. The Linear Pool's accounting and rate behavior could be combined with Balancer flash swaps to amplify rounding effects. An attacker could manipulate the pool state around a rounding boundary, causing the effective rate to move in an economically favorable direction. The vulnerability was disclosed through Immunefi on August 11, 2023. Balancer immediately began mitigation, affected liquidity was withdrawn, and the vulnerable Linear Pool technology was subsequently sunset. Balancer paid a $1 million USDC bounty to the whitehat researcher.
