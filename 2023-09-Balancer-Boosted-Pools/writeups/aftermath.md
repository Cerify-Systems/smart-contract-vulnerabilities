# Aftermath
The vulnerability was disclosed by a whitehat through Immunefi on August 11, 2023. Balancer rapidly implemented mitigation measures and provided a withdrawal path for affected liquidity. Immunefi reported that the vast majority of funds at risk were withdrawn within 48 hours. Balancer subsequently sunset Linear Pools and began developing a next-generation Boosted Pool architecture. The whitehat received a $1,000,000 USDC bounty. The incident demonstrated how flash liquidity can turn a relatively small rounding discrepancy in DeFi accounting into a critical economic vulnerability.
