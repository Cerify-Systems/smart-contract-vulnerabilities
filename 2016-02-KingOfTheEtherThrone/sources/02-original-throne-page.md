# Original Throne Page

The King of the Ether Throne website describes the rules of the game, the history of previous monarchs, claim prices, and the mechanics used for claiming the throne.

Players became the new monarch by paying the current claim price. The previous monarch was expected to receive compensation automatically, while the claim price increased for the next challenger.

This page serves as the official historical record of the project and provides context for understanding how the payment mechanism worked before and after the reported vulnerability.
