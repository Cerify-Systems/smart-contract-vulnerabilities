# Aftermath

PancakeBunny confirmed the flash-loan exploit on 19 May 2021 and paused deposits/withdrawals for affected vault functionality.

Contemporary technical reporting recorded approximately 114,631 BNB/WBNB transferred to an attacker-controlled address and approximately 6.97M BUNNY minted.

The incident caused a severe BUNNY price collapse as the newly minted supply was sold.

The project later published security and recovery communications. The incident became a prominent example of the risks of using AMM spot prices as oracles in DeFi reward systems.
