# Aftermath

Fei Protocol announced the exploit on April 30, 2022 and paused borrowing to reduce further losses. The reported loss was approximately $80 million. 
The attacker was publicly offered a $10 million bounty for returning the funds.

The incident affected seven Rari Fuse pools, while Fei's own pools were not affected according to CertiK's review.
Later, Tribe DAO governance approved full reimbursement for affected users. Nexus Mutual's claims documentation records approximately $5.09 million in payouts for covered Rari Capital Fuse claims. 
The incident became a major example of why cross-function reentrancy must be considered even when a protocol already has a reentrancy guard.
