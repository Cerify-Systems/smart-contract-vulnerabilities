# Aftermath

Sushi's official post-mortem says the vulnerability was identified on 8 April 2023 after RouteProcessor2 had been soft-launched as part of Sushi's V3 upgrades. The router had been deployed to 14 networks. citeturn0search0

Sushi removed the new router from the frontend and urged users to remove pending approvals.

A whitehat rescue operation was launched by HYDN. Sushi reports that the rescue recovered more than $750,000 worth of user assets across multiple networks.
